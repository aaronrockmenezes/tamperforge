## Interpretation so far

Current P0 safety generation supports the expected baseline behavior:

- Base Gemma mostly refuses harmful prompts.
- The two HF abliterated/uncensored Gemma models mostly comply by keyword ASR.
- DeepSeek judge smoke agrees that the heretic model is highly harmful on the
  sampled rows once parsing is fixed.

This does **not** prove the tamperforge thesis. It only establishes external
abliterated baselines and a working eval path. P1 still needs the local empirical
all-layer ablation, adapter training, adapted-model ablation, and matched
capability/safety comparison.
